Travel Cues — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Travel Cues is built to work entirely on your device.

What we collect: nothing.

Travel Cues has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and makes no network requests of its own.

Motion sensors

The app reads your device's accelerometer, gyroscope, and rotation sensors to animate the on-screen dots. These readings are processed in real time on your device and are never stored or transmitted.

Physical activity (optional)

If you enable the premium "auto-start when driving" feature, the app uses Android's on-device Activity Recognition service to notice when you're riding in a vehicle. This detection happens on your device. Travel Cues does not store or transmit activity data. You can revoke this permission at any time in system settings.

Purchases

The one-time Premium unlock is processed entirely by Google Play. Travel Cues never sees your payment details. See Google Play's terms and privacy policy for how Google processes payments.

Data sharing

We do not share data with anyone, because we do not collect any.

Children

Travel Cues does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
