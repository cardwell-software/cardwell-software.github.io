Last updated: October 13, 2026

Tonight at the Tower is published by Cardwell Software LLC. By playing the game you agree to these terms. They are deliberately short.

The game is free, with ads

Tonight at the Tower is free to download and to play from start to end, on a phone with Android 11 or newer. It is paid for by advertisements, shown through Google AdMob. There are none in your first five runs. After that an ad may appear at a break in play: when a boss is beaten, and when a run ends.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks, before the first ad, whether you are 16 or over. If you say you are under 16 it shows you no ads at all. If you say you are 16 or over, Google's form asks what you allow before any ad is shown.

The ads are chosen by Google and its advertisers, not by us, and we are not responsible for what an advertiser offers. What the ad software collects, and the choices you have about it, are set out in the privacy policy.

One purchase: "Remove ads"

The game sells one thing. "Remove ads" is bought once, through Google Play, and stops the ads for good. It is tied to the Google account that bought it, so it comes back by itself on a new phone or after a reinstall when you are signed in to that account.

It removes the ads and nothing else: it changes nothing in the game itself. Google Play shows the price in your own currency before you pay. A player who is shown no ads, because they said they are under 16 where the game asks, is not offered it: there is nothing to remove.

On an iPhone, once the game is available there, the purchase is made through Apple's App Store in the same way and is tied to the Apple account that bought it.

Payments and refunds go through Google Play

We never handle your payment. Google Play takes it, and Google Play's own terms and refund rules apply. To ask for your money back, start with Google Play's refund policies. On an iPhone, Apple takes the payment and Apple's own terms and refund rules apply. You can also write to us and we will help where we can. If a purchase is refunded, the ads return.

Your saves live on your phone

The game has no account and no cloud save of its own. Your run in progress, your settings, your unlocked Encores and your past runs are kept on your phone. Your phone's own backup, if you use it, can bring them back on a new phone or after a reinstall. Without that backup, uninstalling the game, clearing its data or changing phones loses them, and we cannot bring them back because we never had them.

Updates can change the game

We update the game to fix it and to rebalance it, so cards, enemies and numbers can change from one version to the next. A run in progress normally carries on after an update. If an update removes something that run depends on, the game says so and offers to abandon the run.
